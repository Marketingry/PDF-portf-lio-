MethodHow a winner gets made

The loop behind
every winner.

01

Dig

Reviews, Reddit threads, comments and competitor ads. I look for the exact words customers use.

02

Hypothesize

One angle, one variable, one metric that decides if it worked.

03

Brief

Hook stack, script, avatar and shot direction — clear enough for any creator or editor.

04

Produce

UGC, founder, native or AI. Every concept ships with 3–5 hook variations.

05

Read & iterate

Hook rate, hold rate, CPA. Kill fast, iterate on signal, scale what wins.
